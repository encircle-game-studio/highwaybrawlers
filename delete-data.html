Delete your Highway Brawlers data

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Highway Brawlers is made by Encircle Technologies Private Limited. Your progress lives on your device, with a copy in your own iCloud (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games account (Android), and, if you signed in, a copy in your Highway Brawlers account. This page explains how to delete it and how to ask us to delete the rest.

The quickest way: Delete my data in the game

Open the game, go to Settings › Privacy › Delete my data and confirm twice. This removes your progress, bikes and settings from your device and from the iCloud or Play Games copy, deletes your account and its cloud save if you signed in, and resets this game's Game Center achievements. Leaderboard scores already sent stay with your Game Center or Play Games account (Step 3). Purchases stay with your store account; use Restore Purchases to get them back.

If you signed in

To delete only your account and keep your progress on the device: open the game, go to Settings › Account › Delete account and confirm twice. This deletes your cloud save and your Highway Brawlers account at once and, if you used Sign in with Apple on an iPhone or iPad, revokes the game's access to your Apple account (Apple asks you to confirm with Sign in with Apple once more). Delete my data (Step 1) does the same and also removes your progress. If the game cannot reach our server, nothing is deleted and it tells you, so you can try again. If you no longer have the game installed, email us as in Step 2 and say that you used Sign in with Apple.

Step 1: delete the game data on your device

  • Android: uninstall Highway Brawlers. This deletes your progress, bikes and settings. If Android backup is on, a copy of the save file may also be in your Google account backup; you can delete it in Google Drive › Backups or turn backup off in Android settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: delete the app. This deletes your progress, bikes and settings from the device.
  • Uninstalling does not remove the copy in your iCloud or Play Games account. Use Delete my data in the game first, or remove the game's data in your Play Games profile.

Step 2: ask us to delete the data held for us

  1. Email support@encircletechnologies.com with the subject "Delete my Highway Brawlers data".
  2. Say which platform you play on (Android or iOS).
  3. If you bought anything, include the order number from your Google Play or Apple receipt so we can find the matching purchase record.

We reply within 30 days and confirm when the deletion is done.

Step 3: advertising and leaderboard data

  • Ads: Google AdMob handles ad data. On Android, delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). On iOS, turn off tracking for the game in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Google's ad controls are at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Leaderboards, achievements and saved games: these are kept by Google Play Games or Apple Game Center under your own account. Manage or delete them in your Play Games profile (play.google.com/games/profile) or through Apple's Data and Privacy page (privacy.apple.com).
