Highway Brawlers

Motorcycle racing with fists

Race thirteen rivals down long two-way highways, punch and kick them off their bikes, dodge the traffic and the police, and earn the cash for faster bikes. A motorcycle combat racer for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Features

A 3D motorcycle combat racing game: fight rival bikers with fists and kicks at full speed, and the riders you hit remember you. Ride the Long Run across 25 highways in 5 regions, and races play offline.

Rivals remember you

Hit a rider and they hold a grudge into the next race; spare one and they may ride with you, or pass you a tip before the start.

Punch, kick, take weapons

Punches hit close, kicks reach further, and a punch timed as a rival swings takes their weapon, from a chain to a stun baton.

The Long Run

Five stretches of highway racing with a prize pot that grows every stretch: buy a cheap bike, finish in the top three and ride to the last stretch.

25 highways, 5 regions

Coast, desert, forest, city and mountain pass, by day, dusk and night, in rain and snow.

Your garage

Earn cash at every finish, buy faster bikes, upgrade engine, tires, suspension and armor, and paint your bike, jacket and helmet.

Play your way

Touch controls or a game controller in races and every menu, a left-handed layout, and races that play offline.

Questions and answers

What is Highway Brawlers?

A 3D motorcycle combat racing game. You race thirteen rivals down two-way highways, punch and kick them off their bikes, dodge traffic and police, and earn cash for faster bikes.

Where can I play Highway Brawlers?

On iPhone and iPad from the App Store, and on Android phones and tablets from Google Play.

Is Highway Brawlers free? Does it have ads or in-app purchases?

Yes, it is free to download, with optional in-app purchases. Ads show only between races, never during one, and rewarded ads are always your choice. A one-time purchase removes the other ads.

Can I play Highway Brawlers offline?

Yes. Races play offline, with no Wi-Fi needed.

Can I play Highway Brawlers with a game controller?

Yes. Game controllers work in races and in every menu. On touch screens you get a stick on the left and punch and kick buttons on the right.
