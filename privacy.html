Highway Brawlers · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Highway Brawlers ("the game") is made by Encircle Technologies Private Limited, Rajkot, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game handles on iOS, iPadOS and Android.

What we collect ourselves

Without signing in: nothing. Your progress (unlocked levels, best places and times, cash, gold, the bikes you own, their upgrades and paint, rival grudges, career totals, achievements and settings) is stored on your device. The game also keeps a copy in your own iCloud (iPhone and iPad) or your Google Play Games account (Android), so a new phone can pick up where you left off. We cannot see that copy. Turn it off in Settings > Account > Cloud save.

Optional sign-in and cloud save. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Facebook, we store a copy of your game save (the same progress listed above) in Google Firebase, linked to an account ID, so you can continue on another phone or tablet. We do not receive your email address, name, friends list or contacts, and we do not use the account for ads or tracking. Signing in is never required to play.

  • Sign in with Apple: Apple gives us an anonymous account ID for this app.
  • Facebook: we use Facebook Login only, with Limited Login on iOS, and request only your public profile ID. Facebook app events and advertising ID collection are switched off in the game.

Your cloud save is kept until you delete your account. Deleting your account in the game removes the cloud save and the account straight away and, for Apple, revokes the game's access to your Apple account.

Your choices

  • Ads are never shown during a race. Rewarded ads are always your choice.
  • On iOS the game never asks to track you and does not use the advertising identifier.
  • In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and where local law requires, you can give or withdraw consent for personalized ads in the game's privacy choices.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Remove Ads, a one-time purchase, turns off the ads between races.
  • Delete my data (Settings > Privacy) removes your progress from your device and from the iCloud or Play Games copy, and resets this game's Game Center achievements. Leaderboard scores already sent stay with your Game Center or Play Games account; you can remove them there (Play Games profile, or Apple's Data and Privacy page).
  • Uninstalling the game deletes the game data on your device. The iCloud or Play Games copy stays until you use Delete my data or remove it from your account.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to content suitable for the game's age rating.

Security and retention

Data handled by the services above is sent over encrypted connections and kept according to their policies. We do not sell personal information.

Contact and requests

For questions or data requests, email support@encircletechnologies.com. We reply within 30 days.

To delete your data, follow the steps on Delete your Highway Brawlers data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell players in the game.
